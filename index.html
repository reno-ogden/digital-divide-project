02

Digital inclusion depends on five connected parts.

These first-draft cards show the framework I plan to develop visually in the final website.

1. Reliable internet

Service needs to be affordable, dependable, and strong enough for school, work, healthcare, and other important tasks.

2. Appropriate devices

An old phone or shared computer may connect to the internet but still be unable to run the tools a person needs.

3. Digital skills and confidence

People need practical skills for learning new systems, judging information, protecting themselves, and solving problems.

4. Accessible design

Websites and services need to work for people with different disabilities, devices, and ways of navigating.

5. Human support

Patient and respectful help can keep one confusing screen or error message from ending the entire task.
